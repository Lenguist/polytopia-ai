# 3-1 Canvas, Camera & Lighting

## Prompt

Build the main React Three Fiber scene shell: Canvas with the performance settings from step 1-3, a perspective camera positioned for a top-down-ish strategy view, ambient + directional lighting with soft shadows (optional and quality-gated), and OrbitControls limited for a board-game feel. Add a simple sky or background color matching the Polytopia aesthetic.

## Supplementary Information

- Camera should look down at roughly 45–60° for a readable strategy board.
- Limit polar angle so users cannot flip under the map.
- Prefer a dark navy / deep teal background.
- Shadows should be optional and low-resolution for mobile.
- Acceptance: Clean, stable 3D viewport ready to receive the tile grid.
