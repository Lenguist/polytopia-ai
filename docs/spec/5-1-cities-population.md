# 5-1 Cities, Population & Upgrades

## Prompt

Add City entities to both the pure GameState and the 3D scene. Cities generate stars, have population levels, and can be upgraded. Visualize cities distinctly (larger mesh, banner, or simple building). Implement the basic population → upgrade loop and star income from cities.

## Supplementary Information

- Cities are the economic heart of the game.
- Population is increased by harvesting resources and buildings.
- Level-ups grant bonuses and eventually the Giant / super unit.
- Acceptance: A city produces stars each turn, can be upgraded, and is clearly visible on the map.
