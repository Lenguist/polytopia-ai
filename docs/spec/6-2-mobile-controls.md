# 6-2 Mobile Touch Controls

## Prompt

Implement mobile-friendly touch controls. Support tap-to-select unit, tap-to-move / attack, pinch-to-zoom, and drag-to-pan. Optionally add a virtual joystick or contextual action buttons. Ensure OrbitControls and custom touch handlers do not conflict. Test on real iOS and Android browsers.

## Supplementary Information

- Primary interaction model should feel natural on a phone held in portrait or landscape.
- Disable or heavily limit OrbitControls rotation on mobile if it interferes with selection.
- Acceptance: A full short game can be played entirely with touch on a phone without a mouse.
